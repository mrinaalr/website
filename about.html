Background

I'm from the Bay Area, born and raised in Fremont, California. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at Arizona State University and am currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst.

Activities & Life

Beyond academics, I've had various experiences that have shaped who I am. I'm an Eagle Scout, which instilled in me values of leadership, service, and perseverance. I also played water polo, traveled the country camping and practicing jiu jitsu, and was the captain of my middle and high school robotics team, Tacbobots.

Interests

I'm an avid MMA fan and enjoy following and practicing the sport. When I'm not working on research or projects, I enjoy traveling, staying active through exercise, relaxing, and taking long drives to listen to music and explore new places.
